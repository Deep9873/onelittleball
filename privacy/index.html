One Little Ball Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

One Little Ball is a puzzle game for Android. This page explains what information the game uses and why. It applies to the One Little Ball app published by the developer named below.

Developer: Deep Jangid
Contact: deepjangid70@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

One Little Ball has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress, stars, hints, daily puzzle streak and settings (sound, vibration, tilt) are saved on your device. If you uninstall the game or choose Reset progress in Settings, this data is removed.

Usage statistics

We use Firebase Analytics, a service from Google, to count how many people play and to see which levels are too hard or too easy. It receives anonymous information such as an app instance ID, device model, operating system version, country, how often the app is opened, and game events like starting, clearing or failing a level. It does not receive your name, email or contacts. This helps us fix problems and improve the game. More about how Google handles this data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Ads

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version and app version
  • How you interact with ads, and diagnostic information

We do not receive your personal details from Google. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings, then Privacy, then Ads). How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Optional rewarded videos

When you choose to watch a video for extra hints or extra moves on a level, the same ad service is used. Watching is always optional.

Notifications

The game can remind you about the daily puzzle. These reminders are scheduled on your phone and need no personal information. On Android 13 and newer the game asks for your permission first. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings. We may also send occasional messages about updates through Firebase Cloud Messaging, which uses an anonymous device token for delivery.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Notifications: to show the reminders and messages described above. Optional.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Motion sensor: if you turn on Tilt to move, the game reads the phone's tilt while you play. This stays on the device and is never stored or sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not link usage statistics to your name or identity.

Children

One Little Ball is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
